Questions owners actually ask

What does e-commerce consulting cost?

Every engagement starts with the $495 Growth Diagnostic, which is credited toward any project you order within 30 days. The ongoing scope, rhythm and fee are quoted after that, once we both know what the store actually needs. We don't sell a retainer before knowing what's in it.

How is consulting different from just hiring you to build?

Building is execution on an agreed scope, at a fixed price and a fixed date — like the $2,900 Launch or the $5,900 Growth store. Consulting is the layer above it: deciding what's worth building at all, what to spend on, what to stop, and in what order. Many owners need the second more than the first.

Do you take over the store and run it for me?

No. You stay the owner and the decision maker. We work with your accounts, your team and your suppliers, and we can bring in our own build, ads or automation work when a decision needs executing — but nothing is moved out of your control.

Do you advise on legal, tax or financing questions?

No. We advise on the commercial and operational side of an online store: offers, pricing structure, traffic, conversion, follow-up and priorities. For legal, tax, accounting or financing questions, talk to a licensed professional in that field.

Will consulting grow my revenue?

We don't promise growth or any revenue figure, and we wouldn't believe anyone who did. What consulting reliably changes is the quality and the speed of your decisions — and a written record of what you decided and why, so you stop paying twice for the same lesson.
