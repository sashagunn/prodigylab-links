Questions owners actually ask

What does it cost?

Setup is $1,500 one-time — accounts, pixel, conversion tracking, call tracking and CRM connection. Managing one platform is $1,500 per month; two platforms are $2,250 per month. Above $15,000 per month in ad spend we switch to 12% of budget instead of a flat fee.

Do you spend my budget through your account?

No. Your ad budget goes directly from your payment method to Meta. We never hold or touch it — we only manage the campaigns inside your account.

Can you guarantee a return on ad spend?

No. Anyone guaranteeing a specific ROAS or number of leads is guessing. What we commit to is correct tracking, a clear test plan, and honest reporting on what the numbers actually show.

What if my website isn't ready?

Then ads will burn money. We will say so. In that case we start with the $495 audit or fix the site first — sending paid traffic to a page that does not convert is the most common way owners lose budget.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Management is month to month, with no annual contract.
