Questions owners actually ask

How much does a business website cost?

A standalone landing page is $1,900 and takes 7 days. Launch is $2,900 and takes 14 days. Growth is $5,900 and takes 3 to 4 weeks. Custom projects start at $9,900. The price is fixed before we start, so there are no surprise invoices at the end.

What happens if you miss the deadline?

You get your money back. The deadline is written into the agreement together with the price. It is the only honest way to sell a fixed scope, and it keeps us disciplined about what goes into that scope.

What do you need from me to start?

A short call about who buys from you and why, whatever text, photos and prices you already have, and access to your domain. If your material is thin we write and structure the pages ourselves and send them to you for approval.

Do I own the website afterwards?

Yes. The domain, the hosting account and the site are registered in your name, and you keep full access. You can move to another team at any time without asking us for permission.

Will the new site bring me more customers?

Nobody can honestly promise you a number of leads or a revenue figure, and we will not. What we commit to is a site with a clear offer, a working enquiry path, correct tracking and a fixed launch date, so you can measure what happens instead of guessing.
