Questions owners actually ask

What can realistically be automated?

The repetitive part: answering common questions, capturing contact details, qualifying the enquiry, following up when someone goes quiet, writing the lead into a CRM, booking a slot, and on the higher tier issuing an invoice and taking payment in chat. Judgement calls stay with people.

What does it cost?

ProdigyBot Start is $600 setup plus $290 per month, Sales is $1,200 plus $590 per month, Business is $2,200 plus $990 per month. Minimum term is three months. There is a 14-day pilot for $600 that is credited into the Sales setup.

Does everything work on day one?

No, and we would rather say it plainly. Answering and lead capture start quickly. Integrations — CRM, calendar, Shopify, payments — are configured for your business during onboarding, which usually takes one to two weeks.

What happens when the AI does not know the answer?

It does not improvise. It hands the conversation to a person with the full history attached. It only answers from the materials you approve during setup.

Will this replace my team?

No. It removes the repetitive load and stops enquiries going cold at night and on weekends, so the people you already have spend their time on the conversations that need a human.
