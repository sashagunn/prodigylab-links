The Signal

ALTER IGOR builds slow, textural rooms — the sound of a radio you can't quite tune in. Everything is broadcast from 42.0 FM, a fictional late-night transmitter: tape-warm lo-fi and downtempo, ambient edges, Russian-language vocals used as texture more than message.

It's music for the in-between hours — driving home at 3am, the city humming, a frequency drifting in and out. Cinematic, intimate, built to loop. Each release is another broadcast from the same tower.
