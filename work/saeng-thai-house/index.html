Restaurant menu and pickup

Every dish with its own photo, price and options, so a guest decides on the phone instead of calling to ask. Ordering stays on the restaurant's own service.

Studio concept

The task

A restaurant is judged by its storefront. A guest on a phone wants to see the dishes and prices and understand how pickup works — not call to ask.

What was built

A storefront concept: every dish with its own photo, price and options, a “what people order” section, opening hours and the path to pickup. The order itself stays in the restaurant’s own service — we do not replace it.

Boundaries

This is an independent studio concept, not an order from the restaurant and not its official site; the page says so itself. Food imagery is illustrative and no orders are taken. We have no results for this example and will not claim any.
