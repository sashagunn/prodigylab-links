Questions owners actually ask

Do I need a full redesign or just fixes?

If the structure and the offer are sound and only individual pages underperform, targeted conversion work is cheaper and faster. If visitors cannot tell what you sell within a few seconds, a rebuild is the honest answer. The $495 audit tells you which case you are in, and it is credited toward whichever project you order within 30 days.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

That is the main technical risk in any rebuild, so we plan for it: existing URLs are mapped, redirects are set for anything that moves, page titles and structure are carried over deliberately, and the site is checked after launch. We do not promise rankings will improve — we do make sure the rebuild is not what breaks them.

How long does a redesign take and what does it cost?

A Launch rebuild is $2,900 and takes 14 days. Growth is $5,900 and takes 3 to 4 weeks. Larger or non-standard scopes start at $9,900. Price and deadline are fixed in writing before we start, and you get your money back if we miss the deadline.

Can you keep our branding?

Yes. If you have a brand you are happy with, we work inside it. A redesign that sells is usually not about new colours — it is about what appears first, what is said, and how easy it is to take the next step.

Does the site stay online while you rebuild?

Yes. The new site is built on a separate link that you can watch as it comes together. Your current site keeps running until you approve the switch, and going live is a scheduled step with tracking and forms retested afterwards.
