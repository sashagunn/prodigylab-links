How it goes.

Step 01 A designer at your table

A free in-home consultation — they measure the space, listen, and bring the full range of samples with them. No showroom, no appointment at a counter.

Step 02 The space in 3D

Layouts, finishes, colours, textures and accessories, all moved around on screen before a single panel is cut.

Step 03 Installation

A fitting team in the house, and the space working by the end of it.
