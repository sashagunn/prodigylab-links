About the company

Locally owned, built in Austin.

Wild Basin Pools was founded in Austin by Oliver Moore and is run with his wife, Kari, and their team. Design and build stay with the same people, from the first conversation to the last day on site.

Beyond the pool: outdoor kitchens and covered patios, fire bowls and fire pits, spas, infinity edges and tanning ledges, architectural and pool lighting.
