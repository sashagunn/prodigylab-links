What they are looking for.

The things a homeowner notices first. Any one of them can be nothing — that is what the analysis is for.

  • 01Cracks in walls or floors
  • 02A basement wall bowing inward
  • 03Doors and windows sticking
  • 04Floors that slope or bounce
  • 05Water in the basement after rain
  • 06Damp or musty crawl space
  • 07Driveway, patio or garage floor sunk
  • 08Gaps opening at trim and brick

What they do about it.

01 Foundation repair

Steel push piers and wall anchors to stabilise and lift a settling foundation.

02 Basement waterproofing

Interior drainage, sump pumps and vapour barriers to keep a basement dry.

03 Crawl space encapsulation

Full encapsulation with spray foam, crawl space and basement both.

04 Polyjacking and concrete levelling

Sunken driveways, patios, walks and garage floors lifted with foam injection.

05 Drainage systems

French drains, extended downspouts, regrading and drain pipe away from the foundation.

06 Foundation wall replacement

Removal and reconstruction of a badly damaged wall, with drainage and waterproofing.

Two metros, and that is it.

JLB works in Kansas City and Des Moines — not thirty states. The crews know the Wymore–Ladoga clay under KC because they live and dig in it.

Repairs are planned with structural engineers, and the company publishes a written estimate before any work starts. It is a local company, not a franchise.
