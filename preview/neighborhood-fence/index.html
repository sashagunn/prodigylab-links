Good to know

Straight answers, from their own FAQ.

Permits over 6 feet

The City of Spokane requires permits for fences over 6 ft tall. We handle the paperwork so you don’t have to. Fences are typically allowed right on the property line, but HOA or county rules may differ — ask us to verify.

Scheduling and build time

Typically 2–3 weeks in spring and summer, 1–2 weeks in fall and winter. Most residential projects of 150–200 ft are finished in a single day; larger or complex builds may take two.

Financing

0% for 12 months on approved credit through our financing partner.

Vinyl

Supplier UV inhibitors prevent yellowing. Colour stability is guaranteed for life.

Cedar

Staining every 3–4 years is ideal in the Inland Northwest climate.

What a vinyl privacy fence runs

On average $32–38 per foot installed. Exact pricing depends on height, terrain and style, and gates and tear-out are quoted separately.
