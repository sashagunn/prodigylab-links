Good to know

Answers their site gives, once you go looking.

01 A new fence: the visit is free
Their “Getting Started” steps say: “Book your free, no-obligation site assessment where we evaluate your property and discuss your goals.”
02 A repair: the visit is $195
Their repairs page: “To provide the highest level of service, we charge a $195 consultation fee at the time of booking.” It covers an on-site walk of the fence line, a damage check, a written repair plan with the exact cost and a material list, and a recommendation on repair versus replacement.
03 Four offices
Nashville, Jackson, Memphis and Huntsville, covering Middle to West Tennessee, Southwest Kentucky and North Alabama — and, they add, neighbouring communities in Mississippi, Kentucky and Arkansas such as Southaven, Bowling Green, Paducah and Jonesboro.
04 Not only fencing
Gates and automatic gate systems, decks, retaining walls, concrete driveways, sidewalks and walkways, brick and masonry work, staining and sealing, repairs, and temporary fence, panel and barrier rentals for job sites and events.
05 Wholesale as well as installation
Their own words: residential and commercial fence installation “as well as wholesale solutions for contractors, retailers, and property owners.”
06 Colours they say others do not carry
Beige vinyl as a standard colour rather than an upcharge, and chain link in green and brown alongside galvanised and black.
