Good to know

Straight answers, from their own pages.

Permits

Elite pulls the required city permits for your fence. If you have an HOA, their approval stays with you as the homeowner.

Warranty

A one-year Elite workmanship warranty on every fence type. Many materials also carry 10-year, 20-year or lifetime manufacturer warranties.

Their own crews

No subcontractors. Their FAQ: every member of staff is an employee of Elite Fence & Deck, Inc.

What they do not do

No welded iron, no power-washing or staining, and no retail sales — they are an installation company only.

Financing

Through Wisetack: projects up to $25,000, terms from 3 to 60 months, APR from 0% to 35.9%, all subject to credit approval.

How long it takes

Their vinyl FAQ: a residential run of about 200 linear feet usually takes one to three days, depending on the site.
