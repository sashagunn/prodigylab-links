From their work

Site photographs

Decatur, since 1997.

Oakview has been building gardens across metro Atlanta since 1997. The company was founded by Peter Coyne, a New Zealander who settled in Decatur.

Work comes two ways: alongside a landscape designer, or straight from the homeowner. Projects run big and small — a whole garden, or one wall that has started to lean.

What leaves the site.

Oakview works to keep its projects out of landfill.
Old concrete→

Crushed into reusable gravel at a recycling plant

Plants and prunings→

Taken away to be composted

Steel and plastic→

Recycled wherever it can be

Excess soil→

Stored and reused on another site
