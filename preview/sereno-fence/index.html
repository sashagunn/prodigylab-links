Materials

Five ways they fence a Phoenix yard.

Grouped by how the fence is built, not by their menu. Temporary fencing for sites and events, and the gate work, sit alongside all five.

WOOD

Wood

A natural boundary for privacy and curb appeal. They stain and seal against the desert sun and keep it going with periodic inspection.

BLOCK WALL

Block wall

Concrete or cement block, customisable in finish and texture, and the option that also cuts road noise.

IRON & ALUMINUM

Iron & aluminum

Ornamental metal: wrought iron for strength and scrollwork, aluminum for a lighter, rust-resistant run.

CHAIN LINK

Chain link

A cost-effective perimeter for yards and commercial sites, galvanised or vinyl-coated.

VINYL & COMPOSITE

Vinyl & composite

Low-upkeep synthetics: vinyl for a clean painted look, composite for wood grain without the sanding.
