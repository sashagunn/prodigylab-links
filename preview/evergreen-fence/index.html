Good to know

Straight answers, from their own site.

gavel

Permits

Fence height limits and permit rules differ in every city here. Evergreen’s own permit guide covers thirty-six cities and counties, and where a city requires a fence permit they pull it and fold the fee into the written price.

description

Free written estimate

They walk the property, measure the line and flag anything that affects cost — slopes, tear-out, tight access. One written price, with materials spelled out, and no allowances that grow after you sign.

verified

A written workmanship warranty

They list one on every install, and put it in writing. The terms are not published, so none are stated here: ask for the wording before you sign.

construction

One crew for the whole perimeter

The fence line, the gates in it and the opener on the driveway. They come from the gate and access-control trade, so the moving parts get the same attention as the posts.

shield

Licensed on both sides of the river

Contractor licensing, bonding and insurance in Oregon and Washington. Certificates of insurance are available on request for commercial and HOA work.

water_drop

Built for Northwest weather

Posts go below the rot line in concrete, wind-exposed runs get tighter spacing, and hardware is galvanized or stainless.
