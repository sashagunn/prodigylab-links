Elegant design. Reliable value. Excellent craftsmanship.

Dallas, since 2004.

Kitchen Design Concepts remodels kitchens, bathrooms, outdoor living spaces, whole homes and additions across the Dallas metroplex. Design, demolition and installation are one job here, not three trades to coordinate yourself.

There are two ways in. A full remodel, designed, managed and delivered from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. Or Revive — for a room whose layout already works, refreshed without the cost of a full remodel.

Two routes.

Route A

Full remodel

Designed, managed and delivered from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

  • The layout is open to change.
  • Cabinets, counters, appliances and lighting are chosen together.
  • Can run alongside a bathroom, an addition or a whole-home project.
Route B

Revive

Their own service for a room whose layout already works.

  • Refreshes how the room looks and feels, without the cost of a full remodel.
  • Offered for kitchens, bathrooms and other spaces.
  • The layout stays where it is.
