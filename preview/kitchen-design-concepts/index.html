That line is the company's own. Everything on this page comes from what they publish; nothing has been added to it.

Kitchen Design Concepts remodels kitchens, bathrooms, outdoor kitchens and whole homes in Dallas and the surrounding area. Design, demolition and installation are published as one turnkey job rather than three separate trades to coordinate.

Alongside a full kitchen remodel the company publishes a lighter route of its own, Kitchen Revive, and sells cabinets as a service in their own right. Additions and ‘other spaces’ round out the list.

What the company does not publish is a price, a timeline, a project count or a named designer. None of those appear here either. The first real conversation is theirs to have, and their own process page is linked below.
