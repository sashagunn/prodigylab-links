Questions before you commit

How much does a Shopify redesign cost?

The Growth rebuild is $5,900 for an existing store with up to 100 products: custom design, integrations and conversion work, delivered in 3–4 weeks. Bigger or more complex rebuilds are Custom, from $9,900. A single campaign landing page is $1,900 in 7 days. The number is fixed before we start.

Will I lose my orders, customers or SEO?

No. The rebuild happens on a copy of the theme inside your own store, so orders, customers and product data are untouched while we work. URLs stay where they are or get redirected, and the switch to the new design happens in one step once you've signed it off.

Do I need an audit before the redesign?

Not always, but it's the safer order. The $495 Growth Diagnostic tells you which parts of the store are actually losing money, so the rebuild is aimed at something. If you order a project within 30 days, the $495 is credited toward it.

Can you keep our existing theme instead of replacing it?

Often yes. If the theme is well built and up to date, rebuilding the pages, structure and product templates inside it is faster and cheaper than switching. If the theme is the reason the store is slow or unfixable on mobile, we'll say so and explain why.

Do you guarantee the redesign increases sales?

No, and treat anyone who does with suspicion. We guarantee the price, the deadline and the scope: a store built around how buyers actually move through it, measured properly so you can see what changed.
