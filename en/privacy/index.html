Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1.Who we are and what this document covers

Prodigy LAB is an e-commerce and creative studio serving entrepreneurs worldwide. The website and services are provided by Platonaire LLC, registered in the State of California, USA.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data we receive when you submit a request or use our website and ads, why we need it, how we store it, and what rights you have. By leaving your contact details, you agree to the terms below.

2.What data we collect

We only collect what we need to contact you and deliver our service:

  • Contact details you provide yourself: name, email address, phone number — through website forms as well as through lead forms in Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram).
  • Your message: comments and any description of your task or niche that you add to your request.
  • Messenger and chat conversations: messages you send us via WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram Direct, Facebook Messenger or the on-site chat — including through the "Message us" / "Message the assistant" buttons and our chat assistant, ProdigyBot. We keep the conversation history so we can reply and follow up on your request.
  • Technical data: cookies, IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visited — collected automatically through analytics tools to understand how the site performs.

We do not request and do not knowingly collect payment details, ID documents or other sensitive information through website forms.

3.How we use your data

We use the information we receive for the following purposes:

  • to contact you and run a free audit or review of your project;
  • to prepare and send a proposal, quote or helpful materials at your request;
  • to answer your questions and support you throughout our work together;
  • to improve our website, services and ad materials based on anonymized statistics.

We do not send bulk mailings without your consent, and we always give you the option to unsubscribe.

4.Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

Some data is processed by trusted services that help us operate — strictly to the extent needed to deliver the service:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram) — processing lead forms and serving ads;
  • analytics services (for example, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) — traffic and ad-performance statistics;
  • email and lead-storage services — to contact you and manage correspondence;
  • messengers (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook Messenger) — receiving and managing conversations; WhatsApp and Messenger are owned by Meta Platforms and process messages under their own terms;
  • the on-site chat service (Chatwoot) — receiving and storing messages sent through the ProdigyBot chat assistant.

These companies process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5.Data storage and protection

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or until you ask us to delete it. Access is limited to studio staff who need it for their work.

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access, loss or disclosure. That said, no method of transmitting data over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

6.Your rights

With respect to your personal data, you have the right to:

  • request what data about you we hold;
  • correct inaccurate or outdated data;
  • ask us to fully delete your data from our systems;
  • withdraw your consent to processing and opt out of mailings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at platonairellc@gmail.com — we will respond and fulfill your request within a reasonable time.

7.Cookies

The website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and measure ad performance. Most browsers let you disable cookies in their settings — but if you do, some site features may not work correctly.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if our services or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page, with the last-updated date shown at the top. We will try to highlight any significant changes clearly.
