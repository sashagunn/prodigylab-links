Why a first store usually stalls

The build never ends

Hourly work has no reason to finish. Every new idea becomes another week, and the launch date keeps moving until the season you were building for is over.

It looks fine, it doesn't sell

A pretty homepage is not a store. If the product page doesn't answer the buyer's next question, the cart stays empty no matter how good the theme looked in the demo.

It goes live half-wired

No pixel, broken shipping rules, a checkout that fights a phone. You start spending on traffic and have no idea which part is leaking.

Questions owners actually ask

How much does a new Shopify store cost?

Launch is $2,900 for a new business with up to 25 products, delivered in 14 days. Growth is $5,900 for a bigger catalogue of up to 100 products with custom design, integrations and conversion work, delivered in 3–4 weeks. Custom builds start from $9,900. An extra landing page is $1,900 and takes 7 days. The price is agreed before we start and does not move.

What happens if you miss the deadline?

You get your money back. The deadline is written into the agreement together with the price, so the risk of a slow build sits with us, not with you.

Do I need my own photos and product texts?

Photos and product information help, and the more you have the faster the build goes. If they are missing or weak we'll tell you before we start, and we can prepare product copy and creative separately rather than pretending a store can sell without them.

Who owns the store when it's finished?

You do. The store is built inside your own Shopify account, on your billing, with your domain. You keep full admin access and can move to any other team at any time.

Will the store bring me sales straight away?

No one can honestly promise that, and we don't. What we commit to is a store that loads fast, works on a phone, takes payments correctly and tracks what visitors do — so the traffic you send to it has a fair chance and you can see what's actually happening.
