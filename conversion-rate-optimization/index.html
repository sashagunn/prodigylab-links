What a leaking site feels like from the inside

Every new customer costs more

Ad costs rise, the conversion rate stays flat, and margin quietly disappears. The site is the constant nobody is looking at.

Advice contradicts itself

One person says change the button, another says rewrite everything. Without data you are paying for opinions and calling it strategy.

You can't tell what worked

Changes were made, something moved, nobody knows which one. Broken tracking makes every decision after it a guess.

Where we look first

The path from landing on the page to money changing hands, one step at a time. Most of the loss happens in a handful of predictable places.

Questions owners actually ask

How much traffic do I need for this to be worth doing?

Enough that the site is being used every week rather than occasionally. With very little traffic there is nothing to read, and the cheaper move is to fix the obvious blockers and get visitors first. We will tell you honestly which situation you are in before taking the work.

What does conversion work cost?

It starts with the $495 audit, which is credited toward any project you order within 30 days. Implementation is quoted after that, once we know whether the fix is a few pages or a rebuild — a full rebuild is $2,900 for Launch, $5,900 for Growth, and from $9,900 for custom scopes, all fixed price and fixed deadline.

Can you guarantee a higher conversion rate?

No. Anyone promising a specific percentage is guessing with your money. What we commit to is correct measurement, changes made for a stated reason in priority order, and honest reporting of what moved and what did not.

Do you run A/B tests?

Only where traffic is high enough for the result to mean anything. Below that level, split testing produces confident-looking noise. For most small businesses the bigger wins come from clarity, speed, mobile behaviour and follow-up, which can be judged without a test.

Where do you usually find the biggest leaks?

In the same few places: an offer that is not clear in the first screen, a price question left unanswered, a slow or awkward page on mobile, a contact form that is longer than it needs to be or silently failing, and enquiries that arrive but are answered too late.
