Adaptive Site

Industryphotography — the studio's own site
ProblemA portfolio site shows everyone the same thing. Someone who arrives from an ad about sports photography lands on a general showcase and leaves to look for what they came for.
What was doneWe built the site on our own worker and connected a decision layer. It reads intent from the landing page, the ad's tags and what the visitor opened during the session, then picks a pre-approved variant of the block. Rules answer first; the model only refines, and only inside the prepared list — it cannot write copy of its own. If the layer is unavailable, the site falls back to the rules.
ResultLive since 12 October 2026. A visitor with no ad tags who opens the wedding work is shown the wedding variant, and the decision is marked as the model's own rather than a rule's. No conversion figures: the layer has been running for days, not months.

What the cases have in common

The owner misdiagnosed it

In both, the thing about to be bought was not the thing that was broken. The audit is what caught the difference.

The fix was smaller than expected

Neither needed everything rebuilt. Finding the actual constraint is most of the work.

The proof came fast or not at all

When the constraint is the right one, the change shows up in days, not quarters. When nothing moves in a month, the diagnosis was wrong and it gets said out loud.

Straight answers
